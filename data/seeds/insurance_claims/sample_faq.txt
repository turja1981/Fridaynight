CLAIM FILING PROCESS
--------------------
Q: How do I file an insurance claim?
A: File a claim within 7 days of the incident. Submit the Claim Intimation Form (CIF) via our portal or call 1800-XXX-XXXX. You will receive a Claim Reference Number (CLM-YYYY-XXX) immediately. Attach supporting documents: FIR (for theft/accident), doctor's certificate (for health claims), repair estimate (for vehicle), and original bills.

Q: What documents are required for a health insurance claim?
A: Original hospital discharge summary, all medical bills and receipts, doctor's prescription, diagnostic reports, pharmacy bills, pre-authorisation letter (for planned hospitalisation), and PAN card copy for claims above ₹1 lakh (as per IRDAI mandate).

Q: What is the claim settlement timeline?
A: Per IRDAI Regulation 2016, insurers must settle cashless claims within 1 hour of receiving the final document and reimbursement claims within 30 days. Our AI-assisted processing reduces average settlement time to 2.3 hours for auto-approved claims.

CLAIM STATUS CODES
------------------
- Under Review: Documents received; AI verification in progress
- Pending Documents: Additional documents required (list sent via SMS/email)
- Auto-Approved: Claim approved by AI with confidence ≥85%; payment initiated
- Escalated: Claim referred to human adjuster (fraud signals or high value >₹5 lakh)
- Settled: Payment disbursed to registered bank account
- Rejected: Claim denied; reason communicated within 24 hours

FRAUD DETECTION GUIDELINES
---------------------------
Q: What triggers a fraud flag?
A: Our AI flags claims when: (1) claim amount is 3× higher than similar cases in the same pincode, (2) same hospital appears in >10 claims from the same policy, (3) incident date overlaps with grace period, (4) claimant has >3 claims in 12 months. All flagged claims are reviewed by human investigators.

Q: What happens when a claim is marked fraudulent?
A: The claim is blocked immediately, IRDAI FIU is notified if fraud value exceeds ₹10,000, and the policy may be cancelled. The claimant has 15 days to provide counter-evidence.

POLICY TYPES & DEDUCTIBLES
----------------------------
Q: What is a deductible?
A: A deductible is the amount you pay before insurance covers the rest. For HealthShield Basic (₹3 lakh sum insured), the deductible is ₹5,000 per hospitalisation. For HealthShield Plus (₹10 lakh), no deductible applies.

Q: What is not covered?
A: Standard exclusions include pre-existing conditions (first 2 years), cosmetic surgery, dental treatment (unless accidental), maternity (first 9 months), and war-related injuries. Full exclusion list is in Policy Schedule Section 4.

IRDAI COMPLIANCE
----------------
All claims processing follows IRDAI (Insurance Regulatory and Development Authority of India) guidelines:
- Claim Acknowledgement: within 3 working days (Reg. 9, IRDAI Claims Regulations 2016)
- Investigation completion: within 30 days
- Settlement: within 30 days of last necessary document
- Grievance redressal: IGMS portal or Ombudsman within 30 days

AUTO-APPROVAL CRITERIA
-----------------------
Claims are auto-approved when:
- Claim amount ≤ ₹50,000
- No fraud signals detected by AI
- All documents valid and verified via OCR
- No prior disputed claims on this policy
- Hospital is on the approved network list

Current auto-approval rate: 78.4% (industry benchmark: 45%)
Average AI processing time: 2.3 hours (industry benchmark: 24 hours)
