QUALITY MANAGEMENT SYSTEM
--------------------------
Q: What quality standards does the platform monitor?
A: The platform enforces ISO 9001:2015 (Quality Management), ISO 14001:2015 (Environmental), and IS 2062 (structural steel standards for Indian manufacturing). AI checks are aligned with Six Sigma DMAIC methodology (Define, Measure, Analyse, Improve, Control).

Q: What is the current defect detection rate?
A: Our computer vision models achieve 96.8% defect detection accuracy on surface defects, dimensional variance, and assembly errors. False positive rate: 3.2%. Human review is triggered for borderline cases (confidence score 70–85%).

PRODUCTION MONITORING
---------------------
Q: How does real-time quality monitoring work?
A: Cameras mounted at 12 inspection points capture images every 200ms. ResNet-50 model classifies: OK (green), Warning (yellow, <2mm deviation), Reject (red, >2mm or surface crack). Rejected parts are automatically diverted to QC bin via servo-actuated track.

Q: What is OEE (Overall Equipment Effectiveness)?
A: OEE = Availability × Performance × Quality.
- Availability: machine uptime / planned production time
- Performance: actual output / theoretical maximum output
- Quality: good units / total units produced
Target OEE: ≥85% (world-class standard). Current AI-assisted OEE: 89.4%.

DEFECT CATEGORIES
-----------------
- BATCH-DEFECT-SURFACE: scratches, dents, paint chips — cosmetic only, auto-rework
- BATCH-DEFECT-DIM: dimensional error >0.5mm — requires engineering review
- BATCH-DEFECT-ASSM: wrong assembly sequence — production halt, root cause analysis
- BATCH-DEFECT-MAT: wrong material grade — 100% batch rejection, supplier escalation

PREDICTIVE MAINTENANCE
-----------------------
Q: How does predictive maintenance work?
A: Vibration sensors (Bosch BMA456) on 48 motors + CNC spindles collect data at 1kHz. LSTM model predicts bearing failure 72 hours in advance with 91% accuracy. Alerts are sent to maintenance team with recommended action and parts needed.

Q: What triggers an emergency shutdown?
A: Automatic shutdown when: (1) temperature sensor >85°C on motor, (2) vibration amplitude >12mm/s RMS, (3) pressure drop >15% on hydraulic lines, (4) smoke detector activated. Safety interlocks comply with IS 13947 (Indian standard for switchgear).

BATCH TRACKING
--------------
Q: How are production batches tracked?
A: Each batch has a unique ID: BATCH-MFG-{year}-{sequential number}. QR codes printed on batch labels link to full genealogy: raw material lot, machine ID, operator shift, inspection timestamps, and quality score. Traceability per IS 15875 (food-grade) or IS 2062 (structural).
