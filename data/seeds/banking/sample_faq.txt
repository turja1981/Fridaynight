ACCOUNT MANAGEMENT
------------------
Q: How do I open a savings account?
A: Visit any branch with: Aadhaar card (for KYC), PAN card, passport-size photograph, and initial deposit (minimum ₹1,000 for regular savings, ₹10,000 for Privilege account). You can also open digitally via our app using Video KYC per RBI Circular RBI/2019-20/170.

Q: What is the minimum balance requirement?
A: Urban branches: ₹10,000 (regular), ₹5,000 (Janaadhaar). Semi-urban: ₹5,000. Rural: ₹2,500. Zero-balance accounts available under PMJDY (Pradhan Mantri Jan Dhan Yojana).

TRANSACTIONS & LIMITS
----------------------
Q: What is the daily UPI transaction limit?
A: NPCI-mandated UPI limit is ₹1 lakh per transaction, ₹5 lakh per day. For verified merchants (e.g., hospitals, government), limit is ₹5 lakh per transaction. UPI Lite for transactions below ₹500 (offline mode).

Q: What are the NEFT/RTGS timings?
A: NEFT: Available 24×7 (since RBI December 2019 circular). RTGS: 7:00 AM to 6:00 PM on weekdays (minimum ₹2 lakh). IMPS: 24×7, up to ₹5 lakh per transaction. All settled in T+0.

LOANS & CREDIT
--------------
Q: How is the home loan interest rate calculated?
A: Home loans are linked to RBI Repo Rate (currently 6.50%) plus bank spread. Our standard home loan rate: Repo + 2.65% = 9.15% p.a. (floating). Fixed rate: 9.75% for 5 years. EMI for ₹50 lakh at 9.15% over 20 years: ₹45,268/month.

Q: What is the credit score requirement for a personal loan?
A: Minimum CIBIL score of 700 for personal loans up to ₹5 lakh; 750 for loans above ₹5 lakh. Processing fee: 2% of loan amount. Disbursal within 4 hours for pre-approved customers (existing account holders >2 years).

FRAUD PREVENTION
----------------
Q: How do I report a fraudulent transaction?
A: Call 1800-XXX-FRAUD within 3 hours to freeze your account. File a complaint at cybercrime.gov.in (National Cyber Crime Reporting Portal). Under RBI guidelines, you are not liable if reported within 3 days. The bank must credit the amount within 10 working days.

Q: What is two-factor authentication?
A: All transactions above ₹5,000 require OTP (One-Time Password) sent to your registered mobile. We also use device binding and geo-velocity checks — if you log in from a new city within 30 minutes, additional verification is triggered.

DIGITAL BANKING
---------------
Q: How do I activate net banking?
A: Visit branch or ATM to set Customer ID and transaction password. Download our app (available on iOS/Android). Activate via Debit Card + registered mobile OTP. MPIN setup required for mobile banking.
