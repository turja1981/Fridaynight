PRODUCT RECOMMENDATIONS
------------------------
Q: How does the AI recommendation engine work?
A: We use a hybrid collaborative filtering + content-based model. User purchase history (last 90 days), browsing patterns, and product attributes are fed into a neural network. Recommendations are personalised per user and updated every 15 minutes. Cold-start users see category-bestseller recommendations.

Q: What is the current recommendation accuracy?
A: Recommendation click-through rate (CTR): 12.3% (industry average: 4.5%). Conversion from recommendation to purchase: 8.7%. Revenue attribution to AI recommendations: 32% of total GMV. Average basket size increases by ₹847 when AI recommendations are shown.

CUSTOMER SUPPORT
----------------
Q: How does AI handle customer complaints?
A: AI classifies complaints into 8 categories: Delivery, Product Quality, Return/Refund, Payment, Account, Technical, Pricing, Other. Resolution rate at first contact: 87.4%. Average resolution time: 4 minutes (AI) vs 23 minutes (human agent). Human escalation triggered for: fraud complaints, legal threats, or customer sentiment score <2/5.

Q: What is the return policy?
A: 30-day return window for most products. Electronics: 10 days. Food/hygiene: no return. Return initiation via app: instant QR code generated. Pickup scheduled within 48 hours. Refund to original payment mode within 5–7 business days (UPI: same day). GST compliance per Invoice Management System (IMS) portal.

CART & CHECKOUT
---------------
Q: What causes cart abandonment?
A: Top reasons tracked by AI: (1) unexpected shipping cost shown at checkout (43%), (2) forced account creation (28%), (3) payment failure (15%), (4) slow page load >3 seconds (9%), (5) out-of-stock at checkout (5%). Our AI sends cart recovery email/push notification after 30 minutes with optional 5% discount coupon.

Q: What payment methods are supported?
A: UPI (PhonePe, GPay, Paytm, BHIM), Credit/Debit cards (Visa, Mastercard, RuPay), Net banking (42 banks), EMI (0% for 3 months on orders >₹5,000 with HDFC/ICICI/SBI cards), Buy Now Pay Later (Lazypay, Simpl), Cash on Delivery (orders <₹10,000). All transactions PCI-DSS Level 1 compliant.

INVENTORY MANAGEMENT
---------------------
Q: How does AI predict stockouts?
A: We use ARIMA + XGBoost model trained on 24 months of sales data, seasonality (festivals: Diwali +340%, Dussehra +180%, Eid +220%), weather patterns, and competitor pricing. Stock replenishment alerts triggered 7 days before projected stockout. Safety stock = 1.5× average daily sales × lead time.

Q: What is the current inventory turnover?
A: Category-wise inventory turnover:
- Electronics: 12× per year (excellent)
- Apparel: 8× per year (good)
- Home & Kitchen: 6× per year (average)
- Grocery: 52× per year (high velocity)
Overall: 9.3× per year vs industry benchmark of 6×.

PRICING & PROMOTIONS
---------------------
Q: How does dynamic pricing work?
A: AI adjusts prices ±15% every 4 hours based on: competitor price monitoring (50 competitors tracked), demand elasticity, inventory levels, margin floor (minimum 8% gross margin enforced). Price changes require human approval for: brand-protected items, items showing >10% price history jump.
